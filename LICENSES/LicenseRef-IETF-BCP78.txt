Portions of RFCs under BCP 78 and the IETF Trust's Legal Provisions

This license applies to test vectors copied from RFCs whose notices put
them under BCP 78, which the Trust's Code Components license (Legal
Provisions, Section 4) doesn't cover. Their notices take two forms. An RFC
of 2005 is "subject to the rights, licenses and restrictions contained in
BCP 78". A later RFC is "subject to BCP 78 and the IETF Trust's Legal
Provisions Relating to IETF Documents" (https://trustee.ietf.org/license-info).
The RFCs are of two kinds:

- RFCs of the IRTF Document Stream, published after December 28, 2009.
  Section 8.g of the Legal Provisions applies the Legal Provisions to
  them, but says Section 4 "shall not apply to documents in the IRTF
  Document Stream".
- RFCs published before the Legal Provisions took effect, which Section
  2.c leaves under the copyright policy of their day (RFC 3978).

Every value is copied unchanged. Only the layout differs from the RFC:
the values are written as lowercase hex without the RFC's spaces and line
breaks, inside a JSON wrapper that names each field as the RFC does.

Each file under this license names the RFC it is taken from, and keeps
that RFC's copyright notice and legends. The terms are those in effect
when that RFC was published.

For RFC 4231 (December 2005), they are those of BCP 78 as RFC 3978 stated
it, and the RFC itself says: "The test vectors provided in this document
may be used for conformance testing."

For the IRTF Stream RFCs published from March 25, 2015, they are the
Legal Provisions version 5.0 ("TLP 5"), as published by the IETF Trust at
https://trustee.ietf.org/documents/trust-legal-provisions/tlp-5/ and read
on 2026-11-26. The IRTF Stream is one of the Alternate Streams (Section
8.a). For those, Section 8.c.iii removes the IETF Standards Process limit
from the grant of Section 3.a, and says that Section 3.c doesn't apply.
The grant is Section 3.a read without that limit: to copy, publish,
display and distribute the document in whole or in part, to translate it,
and to modify it or prepare derivative works of it unless a legend under
Section 6.c disallows that. The RFCs used here carry no such legend. The
attribution and legends are kept as good practice, not because the grant
requires them. Sections 3.a, 8.c.iii and 8.g are quoted below.

---

3.a. License For Use Within the IETF Standards Process. The IETF Trust
hereby grants to each participant in the IETF Standards Process, to the
greatest extent that it is permitted to do so, a non-exclusive,
royalty-free, worldwide right and license under all copyrights and rights
of authors granted to the IETF Trust:

i. to copy, publish, display and distribute IETF Contributions and IETF
Documents, in whole or in part, as part of the IETF Standards Process,
and

ii. to translate IETF Contributions and IETF Documents, in whole or part,
into languages other than English as part of the IETF Standards Process,
and

iii. unless explicitly disallowed in the notices contained in an IETF
Contribution or IETF Document (as specified in Section 6.c below), to
modify or prepare derivative works of such IETF Contributions or IETF
Documents, in whole or in part, as part of the IETF Standards Process.

8.c.iii. The license grant in Section 3.a of these Legal Provisions with
respect to Alternate Stream documents shall not be limited to the IETF
Standards Process, and all references to the IETF Standards Process in
Section 3.a shall be omitted with respect to licenses of Alternate Stream
documents, and correspondingly Section 3.c hereof shall not apply with
respect to Alternate Stream documents.

8.g. IRTF Document Stream. Pursuant to RFC 5743 published on December 24,
2009, the manager of the IRTF Document Stream has requested that the IETF
Trust act as licensing administrator for the IRTF Document Stream and
that these Legal Provisions be applied to documents submitted and
published in the IRTF Document Stream following December 28, 2009.
Section 4 of these Legal Provisions shall not apply to documents in the
IRTF Document Stream, and all references to Section 4 hereof shall be
disregarded with respect to documents in the IRTF Document Stream.
